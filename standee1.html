Vision-Language Models for Assistive AI
SceneNarrator
Scene Narration + Navigation · VLM Benchmarking
Benchmark evaluation & LoRA fine-tuning of VLMs for visually impaired assistance in Pakistani environments — the first localized dataset of homes & supermarkets with VIA-format narrative ground truth.
Overview

Existing VLMs are evaluated on Western-centric benchmarks that lack cultural, spatial, and linguistic diversity. We curated 400 real-world images from Pakistani supermarkets & homes, evaluated LLaVA, CogVLM & BLIP-2 in zero-shot settings, then fine-tuned LLaVA using LoRA — achieving substantial gains across all 15+ metrics covering lexical, semantic, visual-grounding, hallucination, and navigational specificity.

Key Contributions
01
Localized Benchmark Dataset
400 images from Pakistani supermarkets & homes with VIA-format narrative ground truth using human-in-the-loop annotation
02
VIA-SPECS Metric
Novel navigational specificity metric measuring descriptive density & visual-linguistic utility for blind/low-vision users
03
15+ Metric Evaluation
Unified pipeline: lexical (BLEU, ROUGE, CIDEr), semantic (BERTScore, SBERT), visual-grounding (CLIPScore, SPECS), hallucination (CHAIR, POPE)
04
LoRA Fine-tuning
Domain-adapted LLaVA-1.5-7B on 650 instruction pairs — improved across ALL metrics with hallucination reduction from 44.9% → 41%
